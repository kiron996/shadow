The story of

Debarshi Debadatta Panda

A journey of learning, dreams, and becoming a good person.

Every person has a story. Some stories are written through extraordinary achievements, while others are built quietly through everyday experiences, struggles, learning, family, and dreams.

Debarshi’s story began in Balasore, Odisha, on 8 June 2007. It is a journey shaped by family, education, curiosity, creativity, and an ambition more meaningful than any job title: to become a good person.

The beginning

Debarshi was born in Balasore into a family where his mother, Sukanti Mishra, became an important part of his journey. His father, Late Chakradhar Panda, remains an important figure in the story that shaped him. He grew up alongside his brother, Dibyajyoti Panda.

From his early years, the journey was not simply about attending school and passing examinations. It was about discovering interests, developing skills, and gradually understanding the kind of person he wanted to become.

Education journey

From school foundations to college life

Classes 1–5

DAV Public School, Balasore

These foundational years shaped curiosity, discipline, friendship, and a lasting habit of learning.

Classes 6–10

OAV Sutei, Remuna

This became an important chapter in his academic life, completed with 75.2% in the Class 10 examination.

Classes 11–12

Jhadeswar International School, Balasore

Here, science, technology, computers, and different areas of knowledge became increasingly important. He completed Class 12 with 76.2%.

Present

Remuna Degree College

Debarshi is pursuing a B.Sc. with Geology Honours, with Physics and Computer Science as minor subjects.

A new chapter in science

Geology helps him understand the Earth—its rocks, minerals, structures, processes, and history. Physics offers another way to understand the natural world through fundamental principles and scientific reasoning. Computer Science connects scientific education with technology, programming, problem-solving, and digital creativity.

This combination reflects something important about Debarshi: he is curious about more than one world.

The creative side

Education is only one part of Debarshi’s personality. Outside academics, he has developed a strong interest in coding and designing. He enjoys creating things rather than simply consuming them.

  • Coding
  • Designing
  • Reading
  • Photography
  • Watching movies
  • Playing games
  • Exploring technology and digital projects

His interest in coding and design has led him toward digital projects and experiments with websites, graphics, logos, posters, and AI-related ideas. Technology is not merely something to use—it is something to create with.

The subjects that inspire him

Computer Science

For technology, logic, programming, and creativity.

Geology

For the opportunity to understand our planet and its history.

Biology

For the complexity and fascinating nature of living organisms.

More than a career

Many people grow up with a particular profession in mind—doctor, engineer, scientist, entrepreneur, artist, or programmer. Debarshi’s dream is expressed differently:

“To be a good person.”

It is a simple sentence with a larger meaning. A successful career can provide recognition, knowledge can provide opportunities, and money can provide comfort. But being a good person is about how someone treats others, responds to difficulties, uses their abilities, and the impact they leave behind.

The journey ahead

Debarshi’s story is still being written. From the classrooms of DAV Public School to OAV Sutei Remuna, Jhadeswar International School, and now Remuna Degree College, every chapter has added something new.

There will be difficult subjects, unexpected problems, failures, decisions, and moments of uncertainty. But a person’s story is not defined by whether the road is always easy. It is defined by whether they continue moving forward.

He is a young student from Balasore, carrying the lessons of family and education while exploring Geology, Physics, Computer Science, technology, and creativity.

To learn. To create. To grow. To help others. And, above all, to become a good person.

The next chapter is still waiting to be written.
